## PBI Report – Margins

Purpose:

This dashboard provides a full view of company-wide profitability trends, cost breakdowns, and client-level margin performance to support data-driven decisions on pricing, package offerings, and operational efficiency.

Main Visuals and Metrics
🧮 Top KPIs (cards and line charts):

    Total Margin

    Margin %

    Deposits Conversion %

    Total Margins by Month

    Inbound Revenue by Month

📉 Cost Breakdown (Stacked Area Chart):

    COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)

    Indirect Costs

    Overhead

    Clear legend and explanation of cost categories

📊 Client Profitability Overview

    LTV (Lifetime Value) Margins – Healthy vs Risky Clients

    COGS Distribution Pie Chart

    Most vs. Least Profitable Clients Table

        Color-coded by LTV %

        Sparkline-style visuals for quick scanning

🔍 Package Tier Analysis (Bar Chart):

    Average LTV Margin by Package Tier

    Insight into how product/service tiers impact profitability
